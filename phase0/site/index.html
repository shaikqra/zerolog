Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Yes. Automatic rep counting needs the watch on your wrist.

Which exercises will it detect?

We're starting with the most common lifts, like bench press, overhead press, rows, curls and pulldowns, and adding more as accuracy improves. You can always confirm or fix a set with one tap.

When can I use it?

Founding members get the beta first, planned for summer 2027.

What will it cost?

The planned launch price is $59.99 a year, with a monthly option. Founding members pay $49.99 for their first year.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Full refund any time before launch. One email, no questions.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. iPhone and Apple Watch come first.

Does it replace Hevy and MyFitnessPal?

That's the goal.
