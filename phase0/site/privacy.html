Privacy

This page covers the ZeroLog website and waitlist. The ZeroLog app doesn't exist yet; it will have its own privacy policy before it launches.

Who we are

ZeroLog is run by Shaik, based in Hyderabad, India. Contact: hello@getzerolog.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you join the waitlist.
  • Order details (name, email, payment), if you become a Founding Member. Payment is handled by our payment provider; we never see your card number.
  • Basic visit statistics (pages viewed, country, device type) using cookie-free analytics. No cookies, no tracking across sites.

Why

To send you updates about ZeroLog, deliver what you paid for, handle refunds, and understand whether people are interested in what we're building.

Who helps us

  • Kit (email list and newsletters)
  • Lemon Squeezy (payments and tax, acting as the seller of record)
  • Cloudflare (website hosting and visit statistics)

These services may process data outside the UK and US.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, except where we must keep order records for tax purposes.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@getzerolog.com.
