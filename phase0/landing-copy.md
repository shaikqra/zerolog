## How it works

**1. Walk in.**
ZeroLog notices you've arrived at your gym and has today's workout ready. It's built from your history, your recovery and the equipment your gym actually has.

**2. Lift.**
Your Apple Watch spots the exercise and counts the reps.

**3. Tap ✓.**
Confirm the weight. That's the logging done.

## FAQ

**Do I need an Apple Watch?**
Yes. Automatic rep counting needs the watch on your wrist.

**Which exercises will it detect?**
We're starting with the most common lifts (bench, overhead press, rows, curls, pulldowns and similar) and adding more as accuracy improves. You can always confirm or fix a set with one tap.

**When can I use it?**
Founding members get the beta first, planned for summer 2027.

**What will it cost?**
Planned launch price is $59.99/year (or a monthly plan). Founding members pay $49.99 for their first year.

**Can I get a refund?**
